# 连接词与寒暄（40）

## 逻辑连接（20）

| 级别 | 表达 | 中文 | 说（口语例句） | 写（书面例句） | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | however | 然而 | However, latency is still a problem. | However, the improvement comes at a cost. | 转折，书面更正式 |
| S | therefore | 因此 | Therefore, we chose the simpler design. | Therefore, the change is backward-compatible. | 因果 |
| A | whereas | 而 / 相比之下 | Retrieval is fast, whereas generation is slow. | Retrieval is cheap, whereas reranking adds latency. | 对比 |
| S | while | 虽然 / 同时 | While the fix works, it adds complexity. | While the results are promising, the sample is small. | 让步 |
| S | although | 尽管 | Although the test passed, I’m not confident. | Although the method is simple, it is effective. | 让步 |
| A | moreover | 此外 | Moreover, the approach generalizes. | Moreover, the method requires no retraining. | 递进 |
| S | in addition | 另外 | In addition, we added a regression test. | In addition, we report latency. | 递进 |
| S | on the other hand | 另一方面 | On the other hand, the cost is higher. | On the other hand, the operational cost is higher. | 对比 |
| S | as a result | 结果是 | As a result, the error rate dropped. | As a result, latency increased by 10%. | 因果 |
| A | due to | 由于 | The delay is due to the queue. | The timeout is due to a downstream dependency. | due to + 名词 |
| A | given that | 考虑到 | Given that time is limited, we’ll ship the core flow. | Given that the dataset is small, we avoid strong claims. | 条件 |
| S | in terms of | 在……方面 | In terms of latency, the new version is better. | In terms of accuracy, the difference is small. | 限定范围 |
| A | with respect to | 关于 | With respect to quality, we need more data. | With respect to safety, we add guardrails. | 正式 |
| S | for instance | 例如 | For instance, the parser fails on empty input. | For instance, the retriever misses rare terms. | 举例 |
| S | that said | 话虽如此 | That said, the current version is good enough. | That said, the results are preliminary. | 口语转折 |
| S | to be clear | 说明一下 | To be clear, this is only a temporary fix. | To be clear, this change does not alter the API. | 澄清 |
| S | in short | 简而言之 | In short, we need a smaller candidate set. | In short, the method trades accuracy for latency. | 总结 |
| A | overall | 总体而言 | Overall, the rollout went well. | Overall, the results support the hypothesis. | 总结 |
| A | meanwhile | 与此同时 | Meanwhile, the data team is preparing labels. | Meanwhile, we monitor the error rate. | 并行 |
| S | otherwise | 否则 | We need a timeout; otherwise the job hangs. | Otherwise, the retry may amplify the load. | 条件 |

## 寒暄、澄清与自救（20）

| 级别 | 表达 | 中文 | 使用场景 |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | How’s your week going? | 这周怎么样？ | 寒暄开场 |
| S | That sounds good. | 听起来不错。 | 同意 |
| S | That works for me. | 我可以。 | 约时间 |
| S | I see what you mean. | 我明白你的意思。 | 表达理解 |
| S | Let me think. | 让我想想。 | 争取时间 |
| S | Could you say that again? | 能再说一遍吗？ | 没听清 |
| S | Sorry, I didn’t catch that. | 抱歉，我没听清。 | 没听清 |
| S | How do you pronounce this? | 这个词怎么读？ | 发音 |
| S | How should I phrase this? | 这个该怎么表达？ | 措辞 |
| S | I’m not sure I follow. | 我不太确定听懂了。 | 澄清 |
| S | Could you rephrase that? | 能换个说法吗？ | 澄清 |
| S | Just to make sure I understand… | 我确认一下…… | 复述确认 |
| S | What do you mean by …? | 你说的……是什么意思？ | 定义 |
| S | Let’s take this offline. | 会后单独聊。 | 避免跑题 |
| S | Thanks for the heads-up. | 谢谢提醒。 | 回应告知 |
| S | I’ll keep you posted. | 我会随时同步。 | 跟进 |
| S | I’ll get back to you. | 我回头回复你。 | 暂缓回答 |
| S | No rush. | 不急。 | 缓解对方压力 |
| S | Sounds good to me. | 我没问题。 | 同意 |
| S | Let’s catch up later. | 回头聊。 | 结束对话 |

## 30 秒自救组合

> Sorry, I didn’t catch that. Could you rephrase the last part? … Let me make sure I understand: you want the fix behind a feature flag, right? … Got it. I’ll follow up after I verify the staging behavior.
