目标：能发起、推进、反对、总结会议，并能用 Slack/邮件跟进。

## 会议三段式

1. **开场**：`Let’s start with the main objective.`
2. **推进**：`Could we park that and focus on the decision today?`
3. **收尾**：`Let me summarize the decision and the action items.`

## 礼貌反对

> I agree with the goal, but I’m concerned about the failure mode. Could we compare both options against the same criteria?
