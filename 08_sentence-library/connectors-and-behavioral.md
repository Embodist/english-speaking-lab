# 连接表达与行为面试

> 12 条 · 来源：[[09_sources/_index|原始资料]] · 返回 [[08_sentence-library/_index|句子库]]

> [!tip] 用法
> 表格用来筛选和回顾；要逐条掌握，请把目标句复制到 [[10_templates/sentence-card|句子卡]]，配上录音和复习日期。

| # | English | 中文 | 例句 | 提示 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First, I would… | 首先，我会…… | First, I would reproduce the issue and collect evidence. | 连接表达 |
| 2 | After that, I would… | 之后，我会…… | After that, I would isolate the bottleneck. | 连接表达 |
| 3 | As a result,… | 因此/最终…… | As a result, the service became easier to monitor. | 结果 |
| 4 | One limitation is… | 一个局限是…… | One limitation is that the metric does not capture user preference. | 讨论限制 |
| 5 | I learned that… | 我学到…… | I learned that evaluation needs to reflect real usage. | STAR |
| 6 | My responsibility was to… | 我的职责是…… | My responsibility was to investigate the issue. | STAR |
| 7 | I chose this approach because… | 我选择这种方案是因为…… | I chose this approach because it was easier to validate. | STAR |
| 8 | I would prioritize… based on… | 我会基于……优先处理…… | I would prioritize tasks based on impact and risk. | 行为面试 |
| 9 | I clarified the constraints before choosing a solution. | 我在选择方案前澄清了约束。 | I clarified the constraints before choosing a solution. | 行为面试 |
| 10 | Could you tell me more about the team’s engineering challenges? | 可以介绍一下团队面临的工程挑战吗？ | Could you tell me more about the team’s engineering challenges? | 反问面试官 |
| 11 | What would success look like in the first 90 days? | 入职前 90 天的成功标准是什么？ | What would success look like in the first 90 days? | 反问面试官 |
| 12 | How do you evaluate model quality in production? | 你们如何评估生产环境中的模型质量？ | How do you evaluate model quality in production? | 反问面试官 |
