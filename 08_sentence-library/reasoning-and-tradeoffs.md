# 推理与权衡

> 10 条 · 来源：[[09_sources/_index|原始资料]] · 返回 [[08_sentence-library/_index|句子库]]

> [!tip] 用法
> 表格用来筛选和回顾；要逐条掌握，请把目标句复制到 [[10_templates/sentence-card|句子卡]]，配上录音和复习日期。

| # | English | 中文 | 例句 | 提示 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The main trade-off is… | 主要权衡是…… | The main trade-off is lower latency versus higher memory usage. | 权衡 |
| 2 | The benefit is…, but the cost is… | 好处是……，代价是…… | The benefit is better recall, but the cost is additional latency. | 权衡 |
| 3 | I would validate this assumption with a benchmark. | 我会用基准测试验证这个假设。 | I would validate this assumption with a benchmark before committing to the design. | 验证 |
| 4 | Based on the evidence… | 基于现有证据…… | Based on the evidence, retrieval is the bottleneck. | 分析 |
| 5 | Let me separate facts from assumptions. | 我先把事实和假设分开。 | Let me separate the facts from the assumptions I’m making. | 严谨表达 |
| 6 | The right choice depends on the workload. | 正确选择取决于工作负载。 | The right choice depends on the workload and failure model. | 系统设计 |
| 7 | I haven’t measured that directly. | 我没有直接测量过这个指标。 | I haven’t measured that directly, so I would run a benchmark. | 诚实表达 |
| 8 | I don’t want to overstate my experience. | 我不想夸大自己的经验。 | I haven’t used it in production, so I don’t want to overstate my experience. | 真实性 |
| 9 | Could we clarify the acceptance criteria? | 我们能否明确验收标准？ | Could we clarify the acceptance criteria before implementation? | 需求澄清 |
| 10 | I would compare both approaches against the same test set. | 我会用相同测试集比较两种方案。 | I would compare both approaches against the same test set. | 实验设计 |
