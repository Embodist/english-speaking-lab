# 面试训练

原则：**不要背演讲稿。** 记结构和关键句，现场用自己的话组织；所有数字和贡献必须真实。

## 项目讲述框架

1. The goal was…
2. The main challenge was…
3. My responsibility was…
4. I chose this approach because…
5. The main trade-off was…
6. I validated the result by…
7. As a result…

## 不知道答案时

- `I’m not certain about that detail. I would verify it against the documentation or a small experiment.`
- `I haven’t measured that directly. I’d propose a benchmark using…`
- `Let me separate what I know from what I’m assuming.`
- `I haven’t used that tool in production, so I don’t want to overstate my experience.`
- `Could I clarify one assumption before answering?`

## 练习节奏

- [ ] 每周 2 次 60 秒自我介绍录音
- [ ] 每周 1 次完整模拟面试（15 分钟）
- [ ] 每个项目准备 3 个追问答案
- [ ] 行为题用 STAR，结果只讲真实数据
